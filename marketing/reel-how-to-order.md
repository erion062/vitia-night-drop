# Reel / TikTok: "Si porosit në VND" (30 seconds)

Screen recording of a real order on vndviti.com. **No voice-over.** Music + on-screen text only. Do not mention Pizza La Casa.

---

## Music

Do **not** pick a random song in CapCut. Add the audio **inside TikTok / Instagram** after you export the silent video, so the algorithm treats it as a trending sound.

**Best for this video (how-to, no talking):**
1. **First choice:** open TikTok → Add sound → **Trending** → pick a **fast beat with almost no lyrics** (phone-tap / notification / “oh no” meme energy). It should have a **drop in the first 1 second**.
2. **Instagram:** Reels → Audio → **Recommended** / **Trending**. Use the **same sound name** as TikTok if you can find it.
3. **If nothing fits:** search these names (they rotate in and out of trend — if one is gone, skip it):
   - “Oh No” (Kreepa) — classic for “watch this happen”
   - “Original” / “aesthetic phone” / “click click” beats
   - Albanian: a current **Kosova / Shqip trending** sound with a hard beat, not a ballad

**Volume:** music at **100%** (there is no voice). Keep on-screen text big so people who watch muted still get it.

**Avoid:** sad songs, slow piano, songs with long Albanian lyrics over the taps (people read your text, not the lyrics).

---

## Before you record (5 minutes)

1. Record **between 14:00 and 23:00**, with VND **online** in admin. If it shows MBYLLUR you cannot finish the order.
2. Phone:
   - **Do Not Disturb** on.
   - Brightness high, battery above 30%.
   - Open **vndviti.com** from the home-screen icon so the browser bar is gone.
3. Log in with a **demo** name (**VND Demo**) and a spare number, or blur name/phone later.
4. Empty the basket.
5. Address: a public place, e.g. **Sheshi i Vitisë**. Never a real house.
6. Screen record **with phone mic off** (you will add music in the app).
   - iPhone: Control Center → Screen Recording.
   - Android: Screen recorder, sound off.

Record the whole order **slowly in one take**. CapCut will make it fast.

## Shot list (text on screen only)

In CapCut: **white text on a green #00FF66 box**, big, center or lower-third. Not in the top 250 px or bottom 350 px (TikTok UI).

| Time | What you tap | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Home page, green **HAPUR** badge. Hold 1 second. | **S'KE QEJF ME DALË?** |
| 3–7 s | Tap **POROSIT TANI**, then the **Pije** chip. | **HAP VNDVITI.COM** |
| 7–12 s | Tap **+** on 2 drinks (enough for min. €5). Optional: one snack. Never open **Cigare**. | **PIJE + SNACKS** |
| 12–16 s | Green bar **SHKO TE SHPORTA**, then **VAZHDO**. | **MIN. €5 · DORËZIMI €4** |
| 16–22 s | **Përdor lokacionin tim**, pin jumps, type address + “bie zilen”. Speed up typing 3×. | **PIN-I TE DERA** |
| 22–26 s | Scroll to **Pagesa në dorëzim**, tap **VENDOS POROSINË**. | **PAGUAN NË DORË** |
| 26–30 s | **POROSIA U VENDOS**, then the live map. | **NDIQE LIVE** |
| 30–33 s | End card: VND logo. | **vndviti.com · 14:00–23:00 · Viti** |

Intro Canva clip (if you made one): first 2–3 s, text **S'KE QEJF ME DALË?** then jump into the home page.

## After recording

The order is real. Cancel it: **Anulo porosinë** on the customer order, or cancel in admin.

---

## CapCut

1. 9:16, import the recording. **Do not add music here** if you will post with TikTok/IG sounds.
2. Cut loading, GPS wait, typing. Speed typing 2–3×.
3. Zoom (~120%) on every tap.
4. Add the green text boxes from the table. **No voice-over. No auto-captions of speech.**
5. Blur address, name, phone if they are real.
6. Export **silent** 1080p 30 fps.
7. In **TikTok**: upload → **Add sound** (see Music). Same file to **Instagram Reels** → same sound if possible.
