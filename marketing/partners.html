THE OFFER (say it the same way every time)

  • Free. No signup fee, no commission, no contract. They can stop any day.
  • VND puts their menu or products on vndviti.com with photos, plus a free shout-out on Instagram and TikTok when they join.
  • A customer orders, you pick it up, and you pay the business its normal price in cash at pickup. The customer pays you on delivery, and the €4 delivery fee is what VND earns.
  • Hours 14:00–23:00. You call them when an order comes in; they only have to have it ready.

HOW TO DO IT WITHOUT BEING SPAM

  1. Check the messages below, then work the daily batch: 10 businesses a day, food first. Visit the 5 closest in person; it is a small town and a face wins.
  2. One personal message each, from the VND number. Use the business contacts they published (ARBK register, Google Maps, their page). Never buy lists or blast the same text to everyone at once.
  3. One follow-up after 3 days at most. If they say no or don't answer the follow-up, mark it and stop.
  4. When someone says yes, collect the onboarding items on the right and add their products in the VND admin that same day. Then post about them.
